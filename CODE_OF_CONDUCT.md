# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for learners, teachers,
developers, and anyone interested in Japanese study workflows. We are
committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Respect privacy: do not post real personal documents, study notes, or
  identifiable information in issues or pull requests.
- Follow the repository's technical and legal guidelines, including the
  [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Encouraging or facilitating malicious macro behavior, malware, or data
  exfiltration.

## Scope

This code applies to repository spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
code comments, and any project-related communication. It also applies when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this code. Depending on severity, responses
may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project
spaces.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for
example, a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive). Reports will
be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical. Do not
retaliate against anyone who reports a concern.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct. It is inspired by common
open-source community standards but does not reproduce a third-party text.
